# ASOC Summon Ritual — Visual Asset Handoff

These two images are visual references for the approved Summon Ritual direction. Do not implement or rewrite ritual logic yet; review and preserve them for the next integration step.

## Files
- ritual-platform-reference.webp — approved ziggurat/pentagram visual direction.
- ritual-pylons-reference.webp — approved matching pylon/state visual direction.

## Canonical visual direction
The ritual is a necromantic summoning machine: Warcraft Undead Acolyte summoning language filtered through ASOC cyber-gothic industrial design.
The platform is a front-facing ziggurat-like ritual machine, not a floating neon pentagram.
Five identical soul-channel pylons sit at the five vertices and power the same structure.
Pylons must share one silhouette/housing. Their normal active distinction is illumination color only.
Normal completion means five pylons independently awakened.
Accepted Blood Tribute is a full override: all five pylons ignite simultaneously and the entire ritual becomes crimson/corrupted.
Blood Tribute stands for the whole 5-vote requirement, not one missing vote.

## Production dimensions
Main platform source target: 2048 x 2048 px, 1:1 transparent PNG, sRGB.
Outer sigil diameter: ~1760 px, centered at 1024,1024, with 144 px safe padding.
Pylon socket centers: Top 1024,180; UR 1800,800; LR 1505,1700; LL 543,1700; UL 248,800.
Socket outer diameter: ~170–190 px.
Individual pylon/state source target: 512 x 512 px transparent PNG.
Visible pylon diameter: ~400 px with ~56 px safe padding.
Every pylon variant must be pixel-identical in silhouette, center, scale and anchor; only illumination/corruption changes.
Required logical set: 1 dormant, 5 active color variants, 1 complete, 1 Blood Tribute override. The concept sheet contains an extra variant; treat it as optional/spare.

## Active palette
Use a restrained necromantic family: violet, pale/blue-violet, magenta-violet, cold blue, sickly green.
Complete state may use white-violet ceremonial energy.
Blood Tribute state overrides all normal colors with crimson/blood-red corruption.

## Important note about the attached references
The two WEBP files in this folder are deliberately small handoff previews so you can inspect the approved art direction locally.
They are NOT the final production-resolution assets and must not be treated as source-resolution deliverables.
Do not bake player names, portraits, numbers, text, or UI panels into the art.
Keep the center restrained for animation/effects rather than text.

Please inspect both reference images and this brief, confirm you understand the intended modular asset architecture, and make no code changes until explicitly told to integrate.
